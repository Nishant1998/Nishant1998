## 🧩 What I do

- **Computer vision & perception:** I work with image, camera, and radar data, from person re-identification research to a <a href="https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/the-3lc-cotton-weed-detection-challenge/writeups/10th-place-solution-yolov8n-with-pseudo-ensemblin" target="_blank" rel="noopener">10th-place finish in Kaggle’s cotton weed detection challenge</a>.
- **Model inference:** I optimize and integrate trained models for real-time, web, and mobile applications.
- **Applied AI & retrieval:** I build document search and question-answering workflows that show readers the sources behind an answer.
- **Backend & data systems:** I build APIs and data pipelines that make structured data available for applications, analysis, and dashboards.

## 🧰 Stack

- **Vision & ML:** PyTorch, YOLO, ResNet, EfficientNet, ONNX Runtime, TensorRT, TensorFlow Lite
- **AI & Retrieval:** LangGraph, Qdrant, BM25, rerankers
- **Backend & Data:** Python, FastAPI, SQL, PostgreSQL, Snowflake, pandas
- **Frontend & Tools:** React, TypeScript, Android, Tableau, Git
